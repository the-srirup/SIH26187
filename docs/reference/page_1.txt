Part 0 — What you are actually building
A software layer that sits on top of CCTV cameras that already exist. Video comes in over the network, an
AI model looks at every frame, rules decide what counts as an incident, and alerts appear on a dashboard
with a saved clip as evidence.
The single most important framing for your pitch:
Smart cameras with built-in analytics cost ₹40,000–₹1,50,000 each. A Border Out Post may already have 8
ordinary cameras worth ₹3,000 each. Replacing them across thousands of posts is financially impossible.
Our software gives those existing cameras the same capability for the price of one edge computer per
post.
Everything else in the project supports that one sentence.
