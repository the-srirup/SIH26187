Indian Licence
Plate Dataset in the
Wild (Tanwar et al.,
2021)
16,192 images, 21,683 annotated plates. Explicitly
documents why models trained on Chinese or
Brazilian plates fail on Indian ones — font styles and
plate designs vary too much between countries.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.06054
End-to-End ANPR
for Indian datasets
(Nadiminti et al.,
BARC, 2022)
From Bhabha Atomic Research Centre. Found that
fine-tuning a COCO-pretrained YOLO on Indian
vehicle images was more sample-efficient than
adapting a CCPD-trained model. Directly relevant,
and citing Indian government research strengthens
your pitch.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.06657
CRNN (Shi et al.,
2015)
The convolutional-recurrent architecture underlying
most OCR engines.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.05717
PP-OCR (Du et al.,
2020)
The system behind PaddleOCR, a practical choice for
plate text.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09941

Real-world Anomaly
Detection in
Surveillance Videos
(Sultani et al., CVPR
2018)
Introduced UCF-Crime: 1,900 untrimmed
surveillance videos, 13 anomaly classes, ~128
hours. Uses multiple-instance learning with only
video-level labels, avoiding frame-by-frame
annotation. The canonical citation for this
problem.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.04264
Survey on Deep
Learning for Video
Anomaly Detection
Broad overview of the field and its datasets —
good for your literature-review slide.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.14146
