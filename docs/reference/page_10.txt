SORT (Bewley et

Kalman filter + Hungarian algorithm. The simplest
tracker worth understanding.

DeepSORT
(Wojke et al.,
2017)
Adds appearance embeddings so identities survive
occlusion.

ByteTrack
(Zhang et al.,
ECCV 2022)
What you are actually running. Its insight: keep the
low-confidence detections instead of discarding them,
and match them against existing tracks. Recovers
partially occluded people. Read this one.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06864
BoT-SORT
(Aharon et al.,
2022)
The other tracker Ultralytics ships. Adds camera-
motion compensation.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14651
OC-SORT (Cao
et al., 2023)
Handles non-linear motion and long occlusions better.
